Assumption ledger

Every input, stated so that it can turn out wrong. "Resolves" says when and how: a date and a data source, or, for conditional assumptions, the condition that must occur first. Labels say where each value comes from: market a prediction-market price, data an official record, poll or historical base rate, judgment an estimate from the evidence listed, drafted by a research agent and checked by a second. Probabilities show their mean.
